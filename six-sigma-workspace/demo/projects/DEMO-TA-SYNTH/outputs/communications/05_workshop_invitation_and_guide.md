# Invitation: root-cause workshop for Requisition-to-offer

**When:** [date / time] (90 minutes) | **Where:** [room / link]

## Purpose
Gather perspectives on **why** the process performs as it does. Ideas raised in the session are hypotheses. They are tested against data before anyone acts on them.

## Please bring
- One or two recent examples where the process worked well and one or two where it did not. Remove names before sharing.

## Facilitation guide (for the facilitator)
1. (5 min) Purpose, ground rules: no blame, no names, every idea is a hypothesis.
2. (10 min) Silent idea generation: one idea per sticky note.
3. (20 min) Affinity grouping on the fishbone categories that fit this process.
4. (20 min) Five Whys on the top 2-3 clusters. Stop at a cause the system controls. "Human error" is never an acceptable end point.
5. (15 min) For each candidate cause, agree what evidence would confirm or reject it.
6. (10 min) Dot-vote on which hypotheses to test first.
7. (10 min) Agree owners, then close.

Record outputs in the *Stakeholder_Input* sheet and in the cause-evidence register (status: Hypothesized).
