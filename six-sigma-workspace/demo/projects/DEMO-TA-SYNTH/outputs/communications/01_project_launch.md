# Launching a process improvement review: Requisition-to-offer

**Subject:** Starting a review of Requisition-to-offer: what it means for you

Dear colleagues,

We are starting a structured improvement review of **Requisition-to-offer** in Talent Acquisition. The aim is to understand how the process performs today for its customers and where it can be made simpler, faster or more reliable.

## Why now
Between Jan and Jun 2026, requisitions in the requisition-to-offer process took a median of [baseline] calendar days from approval to first offer, with 1 in 10 exceeding [P90] days, delaying start dates for hiring managers and risking loss of preferred candidates.

## What we will measure first
- Time to offer (days): Calendar days from requisition approval to first offer extended - target status: Not set - VOC pending
- Offer acceptance rate (%): Offers accepted / offers extended (offers >=14 days old) - target status: Not set
- Hiring manager satisfaction (%): Top-2-box share on 7-point post-offer pulse - target status: Not set

Targets are **not yet set**. They will be proposed only after the baseline is measured and must be approved by the sponsor.

## What we will ask of you
- A short interview or workshop (about 60 minutes) in the coming weeks.
- Access to existing process data through approved channels only.
- Honest views. We are reviewing the process, not judging people.

## What we will not do
- Judge individual performance or use this data for employment decisions.
- Change anything without consulting you and getting the right approvals.

**Scope:** Approved external requisitions, all business units, opened Jan-Jun 2026
**Out of scope:** Cancelled requisitions (counted separately); internal transfers; executive search
