# Volvo Group Early-Career Leadership Pipeline (deck source)

A 13-slide executive deck for the Head of Talent Acquisition, proposing a new way to hire and develop high-potential graduates (IITs, NITs, global universities).

- Live deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/2ApUuxLcC66UCby323XvG5
- `project/deck.json` holds the slide order and outline; `project/slides/*.html` holds one slide each, with the speaker notes in the `<aside>` at the end of each slide.

| # | Slide | Core message |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Cover | Accelerating Transformation: Building Volvo Group's Next-Gen Technical & Business Leaders |
| 2 | Our Business Is Changing Faster Than Our Hiring | The talent must change before the products can |
| 3 | Meet the Volvo Intrapreneur | Six pillars, each tied to something we can observe |
| 4 | Today We Test Exams, Not Leadership | The legacy funnel covers one of seven needed capabilities |
| 5 | Five Steps From Campus to Flagship Project | The end-to-end funnel and one integrated scorecard |
| 6 | Pillar I: Innovation Hackathon | Weighted rubric for building under time pressure |
| 7 | Pillar II: Business Case and Executive Simulation | Rubric for TCO, trade-offs and decisions when the facts change |
| 8 | Pillar III: Group Dynamics | Behaviour rubric with positive indicators and red flags |
| 9 | Pillar IV: Executive Leadership Aptitude Interview | Behavioural interview probes plus a live teach-back |
| 10 | Deploy Talent Where Transformation Happens | 6 core areas plus 3 proposed additions, mapped to projects and profiles |
| 11 | Pilot in One Season, Scale in Two | Four phases, each with an exit gate |
| 12 | Measured Against a Control Group | KPIs, investment, return and risk controls |
| 13 | Win Premier Campuses, Then Approve the Pilot | Campus tiers and four decisions for the leadership team |

Figures in brackets, such as `[__]`, are placeholders to fill in with HR, Finance and the business units before presenting.
