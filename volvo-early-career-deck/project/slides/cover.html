Opening (60 seconds). Thank you for the time. Every one of us in this room has said the same thing in the last year: the trucks, buses, machines and services we sell in 2035 will look nothing like the ones we sell today. Electric, hydrogen, autonomous, software-defined, sold as a service. My question today is simple: are we hiring the people who will build that company? I will argue that our graduate hiring model was built for a mechanical OEM, and that it now filters for the wrong things. In the next 20 minutes I will show you who we need, how we would find them, where we would put them to work on day one, and what it costs. I will close with three decisions I need from this team.
