Core message: the selection model only pays off if we put the talent where the transformation happens, so every offer is tied to a flagship project. Talking track: The first six rows are the areas where our strategy is already funded: software-defined vehicle architecture and over-the-air platforms; battery energy storage and megawatt charging; autonomous fleet orchestration, V2X and telematics; the circular economy, battery second life and decarbonising our own plants; digital services such as freight matching and logistics-as-a-service; and Smart Factory 4.0. The three rows marked with a plus are additional areas I propose we include. Hydrogen, because fuel-cell and hydrogen-engine programmes need a talent bench that barely exists in the market today. Enterprise AI and data, because generative AI copilots for engineering and analytics on our own operations, including people analytics, are moving from experiment to scale. And off-road and marine electrification, because construction equipment, mining autonomy and marine drives give graduates a faster route to a full product than on-road trucks. The right column tells campus teams which profiles to target, and business units tell us how many seats each project can host. The rule I am asking you to endorse is simple: no graduate from this pipeline goes into a legacy maintenance role. Visual direction: full-width mapping table; core areas on white rows, proposed additions on toned rows with a plus sign.
