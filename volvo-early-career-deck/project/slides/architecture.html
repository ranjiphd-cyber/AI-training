Core message: the new model spends cheap, automated effort early and expensive senior attention late, on the candidates most likely to become leaders. Talking track: Read the bars as the funnel narrowing from left to right. Stage 0 is new for us: we stop showing up only on placement day and engage campuses all year, through challenges, lab partnerships and internships, so the best students know our problems before they apply. Stage 1 keeps a screen, but modernised: reasoning and maths, plus fundamentals that matter now, such as data literacy, basic coding and systems thinking. It is adaptive and proctored, and it is a gate, not a ranking. Stage 2 is the assessment lab, where we watch candidates build, decide and lead, across three pillars I will walk through next. Stage 3 is a deep-dive panel with a business leader. The outcome is not a generic offer: every offer names the flagship project the graduate will join, with a sponsor and a mentor. The band at the bottom shows how we decide: one integrated scorecard, every score tied to the six pillars. The numbers are placeholders we will size with the business units for the pilot. Visual direction: five stage columns with a stepped bar chart above them, shading from light blue to navy as the funnel narrows; scorecard band beneath.
