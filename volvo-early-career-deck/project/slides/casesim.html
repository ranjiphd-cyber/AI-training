Core message: in a services-and-energy business, commercial judgement is an engineering skill, so we test it directly. Talking track: Each candidate gets a realistic case pack. The example here: launch an electric-truck-as-a-service offer for a mid-mile logistics operator. In 90 minutes they must build the total cost of ownership against diesel, think through charging, financing and the CO2 impact. Then the curveball: halfway through, conditions change, for example battery costs jump or a new emissions rule is announced. This is the moment we learn the most, because it shows ambiguity tolerance and decision-making under new information, two of our six pillars. Finally they present a recommendation to two of our executives, as if it were a real board. The rubric weights unit economics highest, because a technically brilliant offer that does not pay back is not a product. We also score sustainability impact explicitly, because our customers increasingly buy on it. Case packs will be co-written with Volvo Financial Services, Volvo Energy and the truck and bus divisions, and rotated each season so they cannot be rehearsed.
