Core message: we are not hiring “good engineers” in the abstract; we are hiring one specific profile, the Volvo Intrapreneur, defined by six pillars. Talking track: This is the person we need in an SDV programme or a megawatt-charging team. Product ownership: they treat a project as theirs, from the customer problem to the shipped result. Ambiguity tolerance: they keep moving when the data is incomplete and the target moves, which is every day in a transformation. Commercial acumen: they know a technically elegant battery pack that misses the TCO target is a failed product. Systems agility: they think in interfaces between software, hardware, energy and service. Rapid skill acquisition: they learn, unlearn and relearn, because half of what they will need in five years does not exist yet. Articulation: they explain, listen and work across cultures, because our teams sit in Gothenburg, Lyon, Greensboro and Bengaluru at the same time. Look at the blue line under each pillar: every pillar is tied to a specific exercise where we observe it, rather than to a self-description. That is the design principle for everything that follows. Visual direction: hub-and-spoke; navy persona disc at the centre, three pillars on each side, each with its definition and where it is observed.
