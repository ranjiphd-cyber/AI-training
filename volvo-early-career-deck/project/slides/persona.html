03 · The target persona

Meet the Volvo Intrapreneur

An engineer who thinks like a product owner and learns like a start-up founder: six pillars.

Product Ownership

Owns an outcome end to end, from customer problem to shipped solution.

Seen in: scopes an MVP and defends what was left out.

Ambiguity Tolerance

Makes sound progress when data is incomplete and goals shift.

Seen in: re-plans calmly after a mid-case curveball.

Commercial Acumen

Links engineering choices to total cost of ownership, margin and customer value.

Seen in: builds unit economics before recommending.

Systems Agility

Sees how software, hardware, energy and services interact.

Seen in: designs the interfaces, not just the parts.

Rapid Skill Acquisition

Learns, unlearns and relearns faster than the technology moves.

Seen in: a 10-minute teach-back on a new topic.

Articulation

Explains clearly, listens well and works across cultures.

Seen in: influences peers without authority.

Core message: we are not hiring “good engineers” in the abstract; we are hiring one specific profile, the Volvo Intrapreneur. Talking track: This is the person we need in an SDV programme or a megawatt-charging team. Six pillars. Product ownership: they treat a project as theirs, from the customer problem to the shipped result. Ambiguity tolerance: they keep moving when the data is incomplete and the target moves, which is every day in a transformation. Commercial acumen: they know a technically elegant battery pack that misses the TCO target is a failed product. Systems agility: they think in interfaces between software, hardware, energy and service. Rapid skill acquisition: they can learn, unlearn and relearn, because half of what they need in five years does not exist yet. And articulation: they can explain, listen and work across cultures, because our teams sit in Gothenburg, Lyon, Greensboro and Bengaluru at the same time. The blue line on each card is important: every pillar is tied to something we can observe in the new selection process, not to a self-description. That is the design principle for everything that follows. Visual direction: 3 x 2 card grid, one line icon per pillar, definition in grey, observable behaviour in Volvo blue.
