9

Core message: the final stage is a structured behavioural event interview with a business leader in the room, not an unstructured conversation. Talking track: By Stage 3 we already have hard evidence from the lab. The panel’s job is to go deep on the qualities that are hardest to see in a group exercise: intrapreneurship, learning velocity, comfort with uncertainty, decision making and alignment with the Volvo Way. Every panel has three people: a business-unit leader who might host the graduate, a senior engineer, and a TA partner who keeps the process consistent. Each competency has a standard probe, and candidates must answer with a specific situation, what they did, and the result. Interviewers score against written 1-to-5 anchors, so “good answer” means the same thing in Bengaluru and Gothenburg. Learning velocity gets a live test: we give the candidate an unfamiliar topic, say a short brief on hydrogen fuel-cell degradation, and ten minutes later they teach it back to us. Cultural alignment is scored against our values of customer success, trust, passion, change and performance, not against whether the candidate resembles us. I am asking business leaders here to commit panel time, because a leader in the room is also our strongest closing argument with top candidates.
