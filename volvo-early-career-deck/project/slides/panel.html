08 · Stage 3 · Pillar IV

Leadership panel: evidence first, then learning live

Intrapreneurship

“Tell me about something you started that nobody asked you to.”

Strong: initiative, ownership, a result

Learning velocity

“What did you last learn from zero in under a month? How?”

Strong: clear method, proven in teach-back

Uncertainty

“Describe a project where the goal changed halfway.”

Strong: re-planned, kept the team moving

Decision making

“Walk me through a hard call with incomplete data.”

Strong: weighed risks, owned the outcome

Cultural alignment

“When did someone very different from you change your mind?”

Strong: lives the Volvo values

The panel

BU leader, senior engineer, TA partner

60 min behavioural interview + 10 min live teach-back, scored on 1–5 anchors

Volvo values: customer success, trust, passion, change, performance

10

Core message: the final stage is a structured behavioural event interview with a business leader in the room, not an unstructured conversation. Talking track: By Stage 3 we already have hard evidence from the lab. The panel goes deep on what is hardest to see in a group exercise: intrapreneurship, learning velocity, comfort with uncertainty, decision making and alignment with the Volvo Way. Each card shows the standard probe and what a strong answer contains. Candidates must answer with a specific situation, what they did, and the result, and interviewers score against written 1-to-5 anchors, so a good answer means the same in Bengaluru and Gothenburg. Learning velocity gets a live test: we give the candidate an unfamiliar topic, say a short brief on hydrogen fuel-cell degradation, and ten minutes later they teach it back to us. Cultural alignment is scored against our values of customer success, trust, passion, change and performance, not against whether the candidate resembles us. Every panel has a business-unit leader, a senior engineer and a TA partner. I am asking business leaders here to commit panel time, because a leader in the room is also our strongest closing argument with top candidates. Visual direction: five question cards with the probe in large italics, and a navy card describing the panel itself.
