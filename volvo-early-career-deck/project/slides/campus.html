13

Core message: the best graduates choose their employer long before placement day, so we compete all year on three campus tiers; and to start, I need four decisions from this team. Talking track: Tier one is a small set of strategic partners: selected IITs, the top NITs and a few global universities close to our engineering hubs. With them we run a Volvo innovation challenge, co-supervise final-year capstone projects on our real problems, and build lab partnerships in batteries, autonomy and software. Tier two covers further NITs, IIITs and business schools through virtual hackathons and talks by our leaders. Tier three is open: online challenges any student can enter, which widens and diversifies our slate and finds talent our campus list would miss. Strong performers in challenges and capstones get a fast track into the assessment lab. Now the ask. One: approve a pilot at three to five campuses for the next hiring season. Two: name one business sponsor for each flagship area, who will host graduates and help write problem briefs. Three: release engineering hours for assessors and mentors; this is the real cost, and the best investment in our future leaders we can make. Four: approve the pilot budget. With those four decisions, we can have the first cohort working on our transformation within a year. Thank you.
