Core message: the best graduates choose their employer long before placement day, so we compete all year across three campus tiers. Talking track: Read the bars as depth against reach. Tier one, the narrow navy bar, is a small set of strategic partners: selected IITs, the top NITs and a few global universities close to our engineering hubs. With them we go deep: co-supervised capstone projects on our real problems, lab partnerships in batteries, autonomy and software, and a Volvo innovation challenge. Tier two covers further NITs, IIITs and business schools through virtual hackathons and talks by our leaders. Tier three, the widest bar, is open: online challenges any student can enter, which widens and diversifies our slate and finds talent our campus list would miss. Underneath is the annual rhythm: launch challenges, run the assessment labs, make offers and kick off capstones, then use internships as a fast track into next season’s lab. Strong performers in challenges and capstones skip straight to Stage 2. The calendar is indicative and will be aligned with each campus’s placement season. Visual direction: inverted pyramid of three tiers, navy to light, with a four-quarter engagement calendar underneath.
