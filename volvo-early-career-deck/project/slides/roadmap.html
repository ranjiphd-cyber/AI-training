Core message: this is a staged, gated investment, not a big-bang change; you see results from a pilot before we scale. Talking track: Phase one, the first three months, is design: we co-write the rubrics and problem banks with business-unit and engineering leaders, and we train and calibrate the assessors. The gate is your sign-off on the rubric and the budget. Phase two is a pilot in one hiring season at three to five partner campuses. The key design choice: we keep hiring a comparison group through the legacy process, so we can compare the two cohorts honestly on the same metrics. We only move on if the pilot cohort does better. Phase three scales to fifteen or more campuses, including global universities, and introduces a virtual hackathon platform so we can reach talent beyond the campuses we visit. Phase four embeds it: a 24-month leadership track for the graduates, linked into our succession and talent reviews, with a yearly refresh of the rubrics together with the CTO office so they keep pace with technology. Visual direction: one continuous timeline bar that darkens phase by phase, four phase columns underneath, and exit gates aligned along the bottom.
