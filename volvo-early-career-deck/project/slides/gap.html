Volvo Group · Early-career leadership pipeline

4

Core message: our current funnel is good at one thing, confirming baseline technical competence and test-taking aptitude, and blind to almost everything that predicts leadership in a transformation. Talking track: Our process today is an online test for analytics, maths and English, then a technical interview and an HR interview. Read down the middle column. Technical fundamentals: covered, and we will keep that. Everything else is either not tested at all, or tested by asking the candidate to describe themselves, which rewards the best-prepared storyteller, not the best future leader. We never see a candidate solve a problem that has no single right answer. We never see them lead peers. We never see them build something. We never see how fast they learn. The right-hand column is the promise of this proposal: every capability gets a stage where we observe it directly and score it against a written rubric. That also makes our decisions more defensible and less dependent on which interviewer a candidate happened to meet.
