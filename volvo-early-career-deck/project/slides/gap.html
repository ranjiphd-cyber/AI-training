Core message: our current funnel fully covers one of the seven capabilities we now need. Talking track: Our process today is an online test for analytics, maths and English, then a technical interview and an HR interview. Read the middle column. Technical fundamentals: covered, and we keep that. Everything else is either not tested at all, or tested by asking candidates to describe themselves, which rewards the best-prepared storyteller rather than the best future leader. We never see a candidate solve a problem with no single right answer. We never see them lead peers. We never see them build something. We never see how fast they learn. The right column is the promise of this proposal: every capability gets a stage where we observe it directly and score it against a written rubric. That also makes our decisions more defensible and less dependent on which interviewer a candidate happened to meet. Visual direction: consulting-style coverage matrix with Harvey balls; a big “1 of 7” as the headline number; legend in the footer so meaning never relies on colour alone.
