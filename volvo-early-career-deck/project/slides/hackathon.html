The Innovation Hackathon: Build Under Pressure

Real problems, real time pressure: we see how candidates build, not how they describe building.

Format

24–48 hours, on site or hybrid; teams of 4–5 mixed across disciplines and campuses.

Problem briefs from live BUs

e.g. safe OTA rollback, megawatt-charging scheduling, battery second-life grading.

Support

Volvo engineers as mentors; sandbox data and APIs.

Output

Working prototype and a 5-minute demo to a jury.

Core message: the hackathon replaces “tell me about a project” with “show me”. Talking track: Candidates work in mixed teams for 24 to 48 hours on problem briefs written by our own business units. These are not toy puzzles: how do you roll back an over-the-air update safely on a truck in the field, how do you schedule megawatt charging across a depot to avoid peak tariffs, how do you grade a used battery for second life. Our engineers act as mentors, which doubles as a recruitment experience: candidates meet the people they would work with. We score five things on a 1-to-5 scale with written anchors. Problem framing: did they reframe the brief around a real user? System design: is the architecture clean, are the interfaces clear, did they think about safety? Prototype velocity: is there something that works, and did it improve after feedback? Teamwork under pressure: mentors observe who carries load and who unblocks others. And the pitch: can they explain problem, solution, impact and next step in five minutes? The weights favour design and building, because that is what the role demands.
