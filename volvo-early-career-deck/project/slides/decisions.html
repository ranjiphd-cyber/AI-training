Core message: close on the decision, not on a thank-you. Talking track: Here is what I need from this team today. One: approve a pilot at three to five campuses for the next hiring season. Two: name one business sponsor for each flagship area, who will host graduates and help write the problem briefs. Three: release engineering hours for assessors and mentors; this is the real cost of the programme, and the best investment in our future leaders that we can make. Four: approve the pilot budget. With those four decisions, our first cohort of Volvo Intrapreneurs will be working on our transformation within a year, and in eighteen months I will come back to this room with the data from the control group to decide whether we scale. Pause, then open for questions. Visual direction: full navy closing slide that mirrors the cover’s rings, four large numbered decisions, and one closing line.
