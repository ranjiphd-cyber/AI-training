Questions come from this candidate's analysis: the largest culture gaps, the derailers most likely to show up here, values and motivator mismatches, and culture-add potential. Rate each answer from 1 (significant concern) to 5 (strong positive evidence) and note what you heard. Confirmed ratings adjust the scores and appear in the report.

Report & export

      
A consultant-grade fitment report: executive summary, scorecard, charts, the analysis for each gate, derailer risk register, interview probes, 90-day onboarding plan, and methodology. Download it as HTML, which opens in any browser and prints to PDF, or as Markdown. You can also export the raw scores.
