# HRM Decision Lab

An interactive, single-file practice dashboard for learners applying AI decision models in Human Resource Management.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or build is needed.

## What it does

1. **Choose an HRM area:** talent acquisition, workforce planning, learning & development, performance management, employee engagement, retention, or compensation.
2. **Dashboard:** KPI tiles, a chart, and an AI decision insight card (model type, finding, recommended decision, human check, plain-language explanation).
3. **Interpret insights:** 3 questions per area. Correct answers get a 😊; incorrect answers get "Better luck next time" with an explanation and hint.
4. **AI stack lab:** Level 1 and Level 2 use cases, plus a box for your own use case. Answer with a single tool (dropdown), a tech-stack combination (dropdown), or by typing your own stack. Each answer is scored 1–10 with a justification; incorrect answers get an explanation of up to 20 lines.
5. **My results:** download your quiz answers and scores as Markdown or CSV, or copy the report.

## Business AI stack (5 categories)

Conversational GenAI · Automation & workflow · Analytics & BI · Predictive & no-code ML · HR-specific AI platforms.

Scoring compares the capabilities a use case needs with what each tool provides. All dashboard figures are illustrative sample data. There is no "Advanced" level yet.
