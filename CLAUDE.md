# Working notes for this repository

## Recruiter reviews: standing instruction

Whenever the TA Head asks for a review of recruiters (or recruiter leads), always include:

1. **Cycle-time table**: lateral incl. FTC requisitions only (exclude conversion, campus and fresher), per recruiter, per team and overall, in bands <45, 45–60, 61–90, 91–150 and >150 days, for:
   - offered, not yet joined (requisition raised → offer)
   - offered and joined in the year (requisition raised → offer)
   - open with no active offer (requisition raised → today; includes roles re-opened after a decline)
2. **Gender diversity**: women share of offered and joined positions, per recruiter, per team and overall.
3. **Vendor usage**: vendor-sourced share of offered and joined positions, per recruiter, per team and overall.
4. **Time-bound requirements**: for each requirement with a target, show target, joined, yet to join, % closed (joined ÷ target) and whether it is closed or not closed.

Style: grey and Volvo blue only. Use light red only for cells that need attention (cycle time over 90 days, women below 25% or vendor above 15% where there are at least 5 positions, requirement not closed).

Use only the data the TA Head provides. Ask when anything is unclear, and never invent figures.

The dashboard's "Requisition review" view reads this data from the `settings/reqReview` document. Keep people's names out of committed files.
