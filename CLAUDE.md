# Working notes for this repository

## Recruiter reviews: standing instruction

Whenever the TA Head asks for a review of recruiters (or recruiter leads), always include:

1. **Cycle-time table**: per recruiter, per team and overall, in bands <45, 45–60, 61–90, 91–150 and >150 days, for:
   - offered, not yet joined (requisition raised → offer)
   - offered and joined in the year (requisition raised → offer)
   - open with no active offer (requisition raised → today; includes roles re-opened after a decline)
2. **Gender diversity**: women share of offered and joined positions, per recruiter, per team and overall.
3. **Vendor usage**: vendor-sourced share of offered and joined positions, per recruiter, per team and overall.
4. **Time-bound requirements**: for each requirement with a target, show target, joined, yet to join, % closed (joined ÷ target) and whether it is closed or not closed.

**Scope for cycle time, vendor usage, diversity, declines and referrals:** lateral, FTC and every conversion to employment (FTC, contract or consultant, GAT, and NATS or ExcelHER trainees converted to regular employment), each reported as its own stream as well as in total. Campus intern-to-employment conversions count as lateral hiring. Exclude talent-pipeline programme hiring itself (NATS, ExcelHER, AICTE/PhD, interns) and other campus/fresher hires.

Where data is not available, never fill it in: leave a clearly marked "[To fill]" field for the TA Head.

Style: use as few colours as possible: grey and Volvo blue only. Use light red only for cells that need attention (cycle time over 90 days, women below 25% or vendor above 15% where there are at least 5 positions, requirement not closed).

Use only the data the TA Head provides. Ask when anything is unclear, and never invent figures.

The dashboard's "Requisition review" view reads this data from the `settings/reqReview` document. Keep people's names out of committed files.

## Feedback on recruiter behaviour

Feedback from HRBPs, hiring managers and the TA Head is stored in the dashboard's `feedback` collection. Each entry holds: person, date, from (role and name), behaviour area, type, details, feedback, agreed action, owner, due date, status and progress. It appears on the Feedback log page and on the person's Behaviours tab (under the matching behaviour), Development plan tab (agreed actions) and Overview tab. Include it in recruiter reviews. Overdue open actions are shown in light red.
